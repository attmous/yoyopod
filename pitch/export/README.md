# YoYoPod logo — Family Loop

Family Loop turns the product promise, “two sides, one family,” into one
continuous symbol:

- The two dots and balanced loops are two people with equal visual weight.
- The unbroken figure-eight is a family connection, an audio signal, and a
  yoyo string.
- The center ring is the shared pod and the axle that keeps both sides
  connected.

The mark is deliberately friendly without looking like a toy. Its broad
silhouette and simple geometry stay recognizable at small sizes, while the
asymmetrical coral and cyan nodes give each side its own identity. A cream
keyline keeps the continuous loop legible on dark surfaces without requiring a
separate logo.

Keep clear space around the mark, preserve its proportions, and do not rotate
it or rearrange its parts.
